## Live checks

All on the fictional FitBox project; no real project was involved.

- **Live retrieval:** `revalidate --fetch-official` fetched GDPR, ePrivacy, the
  Consumer Rights Directive and the AI Act from `publications.europa.eu` (4/4 retrieved,
  200, XHTML or HTML). The report expanded all 10 declared article references,
  including ePrivacy Article 5 from the older unmarked HTML layout.
- **Suggestion quality:** on 10 FitBox requirements with an expected article written
  down beforehand, the expected article was in the top three for 9 and first for 7.
  The weights were tuned on the same 10, so this overstates quality on new projects.
  Misses: cookie consent (ePrivacy Article 5 not in the top three), retention (an AI
  Act article ranked above GDPR Article 5).
- **Grounding:** applying 4 confirmed technical facts cut fact gaps from 23 to 19;
  organisational facts were refused; changing a pinned file made the evidence
  unavailable.
- **Real Claude Code 2.1.318 in tmux (160×50), plugin loaded with `--plugin-dir`:**
  - the band showed `Next: review source target-gdpr · unblocks 7 open requirements`
    and seven buttons;
  - Panel (ctrl+x Tab, `p`) docked the pane beside the chat with three ranked steps,
    19 owner questions by id and gaps by area;
  - `/eucg-next` ran `next`, opened the report for the source step, and refused to
    record a source review without a named reviewer;
  - `/eucg-pr master...newsletter` on a new newsletter branch flagged personal data,
    age, consent and marketing touchpoints, proposed declaration edits and asked
    before writing, without filling in reviewer, lawful basis or evidence.

## Release review

Two read-only reviewers (Python changes; plugin, skills and the Action) reported
these findings, all fixed in separate commits with regression tests:

- `ground --apply` could write through a planted temp-file symlink;
- a bare relative `--input` indexed the wrong root;
- oversized files were read whole before being skipped;
- report rendering re-tokenized every article per requirement (minutes on large
  catalogues); the article index is now built once;
- plain-text acts opened an article at a reference such as "Article 6(1)";
- an invalid language tag was silently fetched in English;
- diff paths could break out of PR comment code spans;
- broad words counted as a concept being mentioned in declarations;
- the band could start overlapping rankings and retried a failed ranking every turn;
- skills could pin evidence or record review fields the owner did not give;
- the Action example could edit another bot's comment, treated a crash as "needs
  attention", put `base_ref` straight into the script and kept the checkout token.
